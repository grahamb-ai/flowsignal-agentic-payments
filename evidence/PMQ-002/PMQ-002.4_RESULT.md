# PMQ-002.4 — Permit Expiry at Final Boundary Result

## Frozen proposition

> An execution permit MUST still be temporally valid when the protected consequence boundary is acquired. A permit that expires while waiting to enter that boundary MUST NOT form the represented consequence.

## Initial result

## Remediation

`execute_protected_consequence()` now revalidates `valid_until` immediately after acquiring `authority_state_guard()`, before current authority-state validation, durable permit consumption and represented consequence formation.

The earlier pre-boundary expiry check remains in place; the added boundary-local check closes the represented wait-time TOCTOU interval exposed by the frozen challenge.
