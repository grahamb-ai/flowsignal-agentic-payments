# PMQ-002.4 — Initial Failure Record

## Frozen proposition

> An execution permit MUST still be temporally valid when the protected consequence boundary is acquired. A permit that expires while waiting to enter that boundary MUST NOT form the represented consequence.

## Initial result

**FAIL — EXPIRED PERMIT FORMED CONSEQUENCE AFTER BOUNDARY WAIT**

GitHub Actions run: `32161234206`

Integrated regression result:

**1 failed / 46 passed**

Observed outcome:

`CONSEQUENCE_FORMED`

The permit was valid when execution began, then expired while the executor waited for the represented authority-state guard. After the guard became available, the executor entered the protected boundary and formed the represented consequence without rechecking permit expiry.

## Failure mechanism

`execute_protected_consequence()` checked `valid_until` before acquiring `authority_state_guard()`. Temporal validity was therefore established outside the final critical section. A permit could pass that earlier check, wait long enough to expire, then proceed through current-state validation and consequence formation.

This is a time-of-check/time-of-use weakness at the represented execution boundary.

## Classification

**FAIL — represented in-process temporal standing was not revalidated at final boundary acquisition.**

This failure is preserved before remediation. The frozen proposition and test semantics are not changed.
