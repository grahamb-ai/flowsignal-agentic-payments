## Demonstrated scope

Within the represented Agentic Payments MVP execution surface, when the executor uses the same available SQLite permit-consumption store across the represented restart boundary:

- a valid execution permit can form the represented consequence once;
- the permit consumption survives execution-component reload;
- the exact consumed permit cannot form a second represented consequence after that reload; and
- the maintained regression suite remains green at 43 passed / 0 failed on the remediated branch head.

## Residual limitations / NOT DEMONSTRATED

This test does **not** demonstrate:

- production database durability or high availability;
- survival of real host/container/storage loss;
- independent multi-instance execution against a production shared store;
- distributed consensus or cross-region serializability;
- external payment-rail idempotency;
- production process/IAM/KMS/HSM isolation; or
- universal consequence-route closure.

Those remain separate proof obligations.

## Strongest safe conclusion

**Within the represented MVP boundary, a previously consumed execution permit no longer forms a second represented consequence after execution-component restart/reload when the same durable reference permit-consumption store remains available.**
