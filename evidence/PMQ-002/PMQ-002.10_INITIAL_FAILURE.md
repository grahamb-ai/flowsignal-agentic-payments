## Classification

**FAIL — DURABLE STORE ROLLBACK RE-ENABLES CONSUMED PERMIT**

## What the failure demonstrates

Within the represented local SQLite persistence boundary exercised by PMQ-002.10, replay protection and consequence-outcome history are not rollback-resistant when both durable execution-state stores are restored together to an older pre-execution state.

The prior PMQ-002.9 atomicity property still addresses crash separation between permit consumption and initial outcome creation. It does not provide an independent monotonic or rollback-resistant anchor outside the two stores being restored.

The PMQ-002.10 failure therefore identifies a distinct persistence limitation: an internally consistent but older snapshot can erase both the consumed-permit record and the corresponding consequence-outcome record, allowing the same still-valid permit/action binding to be accepted again on the represented surface.

This test **did demonstrate a second represented consequence**. It did not demonstrate a duplicate external payment or any physical external consequence.

## Scope

The demonstrated failure is limited to the represented reference-MVP local SQLite stores and the exact two-store rollback condition exercised by the frozen test.

It does not establish behavior for production backup/restore systems, storage IAM, database HA/replication, cross-host or multi-region recovery, immutable external audit, external monotonic counters, distributed consensus, external payment-rail idempotency, universal route closure or physical consequence formation.

## Evidence discipline

This failure is preserved before remediation. The frozen proposition and test semantics must not be weakened. Any remediation must be followed by the same rollback proposition and full maintained regression before final classification.
