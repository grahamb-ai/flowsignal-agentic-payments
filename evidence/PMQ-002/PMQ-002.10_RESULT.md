## Frozen proposition

> Once a represented execution permit has been durably consumed and its initial execution outcome state durably established, restoration of an older durable-store snapshot MUST NOT make that same permit appear unused such that the represented consequence can be formed again without detection.

## Remediation

The reference executor was strengthened with a separate rollback-detection anchor that is outside the two execution-state stores restored by the frozen challenge.

The anchor records the permit signature and action binding before represented consequence formation. When the permit-consumption and consequence-outcome stores are subsequently moved backwards while the anchor survives, the executor can distinguish the restored state from an unused permit and deny the resurrected execution.

The first remediation rerun was not accepted as PASS because it regressed previously qualified behavior:

- concurrent rollback-anchor initialization exposed a SQLite lock in PMQ-002.2;
- ordinary replay in PMQ-002.7 and PMQ-002.8 was incorrectly surfaced as rollback/replay rather than preserving the established replay-denial behavior.

Those regressions were corrected without weakening the frozen PMQ-002.10 proposition or changing its executable challenge semantics. The rollback-anchor store received bounded busy/lock handling, and executor ordering was corrected so ordinary replay remains `DENIED_EXECUTION_PERMIT_REPLAY` while the specific surviving-anchor + restored-execution-state condition is denied as rollback/replay.
