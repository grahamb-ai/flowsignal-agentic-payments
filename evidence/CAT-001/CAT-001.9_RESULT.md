# CAT-001.9 — Receipt / Evidence Integrity

**Qualification date:** 20 August 2026  
**Target:** `Kamanaka5502/bind-time-authority-proof` public bounded demonstrator  
**Frozen target commit:** `d71d743e23098fb58454d8d412cb4a988681d2c1`  
**Classification:** PARTIAL — bounded EXECUTE receipt/chain evidence and replay mutation detection are represented; refusal/non-formation evidence and durable receipt integrity are not demonstrated

## Frozen proposition

> Published receipts, chain summaries, or evidence objects MUST support the claim being made about determination and represented consequence state.

A decision receipt alone MUST NOT be treated as proof of consequence non-formation.

## Public evidence inspected

`boundary_server.py` creates a receipt only after its local decision is `EXECUTE`. The receipt includes index, parent tip, chain tip, corridor, decision, `authority_valid`, phi, admissibility distance, latency and issuance time. A receipt hash is computed over the receipt before it is appended to the in-memory log and JSON ledger.

The same module exposes `/receipts` and `/replay_full`. `replay_from_genesis()` recomputes the deterministic boundary commitment for each stored receipt and compares that expected commitment with the stored full chain tip.

The repository also contains deterministic hashing/signing helpers and a replay harness that detects a deliberately changed chain item.

## What the evidence supports

The public surface therefore provides bounded evidence of successful route-local admission and chain progression. It can also detect some mutations that alter the deterministic commitment/chain representation.

That is meaningful evidence and is credited.

## Limitations

A non-`EXECUTE` result in `/commit` returns before a receipt is created. Therefore the public receipt ledger does not preserve a refusal receipt showing both the refused determination and the represented consequence state after refusal.

The receipt records the server's decision and its own receipt-chain mutation. It is not independent evidence of an external protected consequence.

The receipt hash is stored alongside the mutable JSON receipt in the same ledger, and the inspected replay function validates deterministic chain commitments rather than independently recomputing and verifying each stored `receipt_hash`. The public surface therefore does not establish write-once or independently anchored evidence integrity.

## Classification

**PARTIAL — bounded EXECUTE receipt/chain evidence and replay mutation detection are represented; refusal/non-formation evidence and durable receipt integrity are not demonstrated.**

This is not a FAIL because the public demonstrator does create structured evidence and provides deterministic replay/mutation checks.

It is not a PASS because the evidence does not support the full consequence-state burden, especially non-formation after refusal, and the public ledger is not demonstrated as independently anchored or immutable.

## Strongest safe conclusion

> The frozen public demonstrator provides structured receipts for admitted `/commit` operations and deterministic chain/replay evidence for that represented route. It does not provide equivalent refusal/non-formation receipts or independent durable proof of external consequence state. Receipt/evidence integrity is therefore PARTIAL within the published demonstrator boundary.

## Scope limitation

This result does not assess the target's undisclosed production ledger, signing custody, external settlement evidence, write-once storage, or production audit infrastructure.
