# CAT-001.FS.12 — Durable State Rollback

**Target:** FlowSignal Agentic Payments reference-MVP  
**Frozen target commit:** `4c5092c8df704a58327c4a5e5c6ae9fc81755ef2`  
**Classification:** PASS — exact tested rollback condition with surviving independent reference anchor; broader rollback resistance NOT DEMONSTRATED

## Frozen proposition

If the demonstrator relies on durable authority/execution state, restoration of older durable state MUST NOT silently resurrect authority or execution capability that the demonstrator claims is no longer valid.

## Evidence

PMQ-002.10 directly executed this challenge. The permit-consumption and consequence-outcome stores were restored to pre-execution snapshots. The initial challenge genuinely failed: restoration re-enabled the exact consumed permit and it formed a second represented consequence. That failure was preserved.

Remediation introduced a separate reference rollback anchor intended to survive restoration of the two tested execution-state stores. The first remediation regressed three previously qualified behaviours; those regressions were preserved and corrected rather than accepted.

The corrected implementation reran the rollback proposition and the full integrated regression completed with 53 passed / 0 failed. Under the exact tested condition, restoring the permit/outcome stores no longer re-enables the same consumed permit when the separate anchor survives.

## Classification

**PASS — exact tested rollback condition with surviving independent reference anchor.**

The frozen CAT proposition is actually executed here: older durable state is restored, the previously consumed capability is retried, and represented consequence formation is prevented after remediation.

## Historical failure lineage

This PASS explicitly retains the original rollback failure in which the same permit formed a second represented consequence, plus the regressions introduced by the first attempted remediation.

## Strongest safe conclusion

> On the frozen FlowSignal reference-MVP, restoration of the tested permit-consumption and outcome stores to pre-execution snapshots does not resurrect the consumed permit when the separate reference rollback anchor survives.

## Residual limitation

Rollback where the separate anchor is also restored, deleted, corrupted or compromised remains NOT DEMONSTRATED. Production external monotonic counters, immutable ledgers, privileged-storage compromise resistance and full disaster-recovery rollback protection are not established.
